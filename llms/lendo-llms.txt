# Lendo — Debt-Based Crowdfunding Platform for SMEs in Saudi Arabia


> Lendo is a Saudi fintech company licensed by the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA), specializing in debt-based crowdfunding. It connects SMEs that need liquidity with individual and institutional investors through fully digital, Sharia-compliant financing solutions.


License No: 61/A SH/202203 — Capital: SAR 50M — Headquartered in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
